# 🌱 EcoPoints by group Fantastic 4

**Turning Food Waste into Rewards for a Greener Community**

## 📖 About the Project

EcoPoints is a sustainability initiative designed to encourage residents to recycle their food waste by rewarding them with points that can be redeemed to offset selected facility or essential bills.

The system aims to reduce food waste sent to landfills, promote environmentally responsible habits, and create a circular economy within the residential community managed by **Sime Darby Property**.

Through EcoPoints, residents can contribute to a greener environment while receiving tangible benefits for their efforts.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- ♻️ Reduce the amount of food waste sent to landfills.
- 🌱 Convert collected food waste into organic fertiliser.
- 🏘️ Encourage residents to practise sustainable waste management.
- 🎁 Reward residents based on the amount of food waste they contribute.
- 💰 Generate revenue from organic fertiliser sales to support the reward system and community facilities.
- 🌍 Establish a sustainable, community-driven circular economy.

More, Grow Greener.**
