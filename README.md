# 🌱 EcoPoints by group Fantastic 4

**Turning Food Waste into Rewards for a Greener Community**

## 📖 About the Project

EcoPoints is a sustainability initiative designed to encourage residents to recycle their food waste by rewarding them with points that can be redeemed to offset selected facility or essential bills.

The system aims to reduce food waste sent to landfills, promote environmentally responsible habits, and create a circular economy within the residential community managed by **Sime Darby Property**.

Through EcoPoints, residents can contribute to a greener environment while receiving tangible benefits for their efforts.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- ♻️ Reduce the amount of food waste sent to landfills.
- 🌱 Convert collected food waste into organic fertiliser.
- 🏘️ Encourage residents to practise sustainable waste management.
- 🎁 Reward residents based on the amount of food waste they contribute.
- 💰 Generate revenue from organic fertiliser sales to support the reward system and community facilities.
- 🌍 Establish a sustainable, community-driven circular economy.

## 🔄 How It Works

### 1. Food Waste Collection

Residents collect their household food waste and bring it to a designated **Food Waste Collection Centre** within their residential block.

The final location of the collection centre is still under consideration:
- One collection centre on each residential floor.
- One centralised collection centre on the lowest floor of each block.

### 2. Food Waste Weighing

A designated worker at the collection centre weighs the food waste brought by each resident.

The worker then informs the resident of the recorded weight.

### 3. Weight Submission

The resident logs in to the EcoPoints website and submits the recorded weight of their food waste.

The system associates the submission with the resident's account.

### 4. Worker Verification

The worker accesses the EcoPoints verification system to check the resident's submitted information against the measured weight.

If the submission is verified, the transaction is approved. This step helps ensure that the points awarded correspond to the actual weight of food waste collected.

### 5. EcoPoints Reward

Once the submission has been verified, the resident receives points according to the following conversion rate:

**1 kg of food waste = 1 EcoPoint**

For example:

| Food Waste Contributed | EcoPoints Earned |
|---:|---:|
| 1 kg | 1 point |
| 5 kg | 5 points |
| 10 kg | 10 points |
| 20 kg | 20 points |

The accumulated points are displayed on the resident's EcoPoints account.

### 6. Monthly Bill Redemption

At the end of the month, residents can use their accumulated EcoPoints to offset eligible facility or essential bills of their choice, subject to the redemption rules established by the property management.

The final monetary value of each point and the list of eligible bills will be determined by the management.

### 7. Transportation to the Fertiliser Facility

The collected food waste is gathered by the workers and transported to a designated fertiliser processing facility.

Transportation is planned to take place at regular intervals, potentially several times a day, depending on the volume of waste collected and the operational requirements.

### 8. Organic Fertiliser Production

At the processing facility, the collected food waste is converted into organic fertiliser.

This process helps divert organic waste from landfills and creates a useful product that can support sustainable gardening and agriculture.

### 9. Fertiliser Sales and Revenue Distribution

Sime Darby Property management will oversee the sale of the organic fertiliser produced from the collected food waste.

The proposed revenue allocation model is:

- **EcoPoints rewards:** RM5 in eligible fertiliser sales revenue = 1 EcoPoint allocated to the reward pool.
- **Community facilities:** Any remaining profit after the agreed reward allocation and other applicable costs will be channelled towards maintaining or improving community facilities.

The final allocation method, operating costs, and financial reconciliation process will need to be confirmed by management.

### 10. A Continuous Sustainability Cycle

The process repeats as residents continue contributing food waste, earning EcoPoints, redeeming rewards, and supporting the production of organic fertiliser.

This creates a circular system in which household food waste becomes a valuable resource that benefits both residents and the wider community.

## 🖥️ Core System Features

The EcoPoints website is intended to provide the following features:

- **Resident Account:** Allows residents to access their accounts and view their EcoPoints balance.
- **Food Waste Submission:** Allows residents to submit the weight recorded at the collection centre.
- **Weight Verification:** Enables workers to verify submitted weights before rewards are issued.
- **Points Tracking:** Records approved food waste contributions and updates residents' point balances.
- **Redemption System:** Allows residents to redeem points against eligible bills.
- **Transaction History:** Maintains records of food waste submissions, verification status, points earned, and redemption activity.
- **Worker Dashboard:** Allows authorised workers to review resident submissions and manage collection records.
- **Management Dashboard:** Provides management with an overview of waste collected, points distributed, redemptions, fertiliser production, and sales revenue.

These features represent the proposed system scope and may be refined during development.

## 👥 System Users

| User | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Residents | Submit food waste weights, track EcoPoints, and redeem rewards. |
| Collection Centre Workers | Weigh food waste, verify submissions, and manage collection records. |
| Property Management | Manage the reward system, monitor operations, oversee fertiliser sales, and allocate revenue. |
| Fertiliser Processing Facility | Receive collected food waste and process it into organic fertiliser. |

## 💡 Proposed Point System

EcoPoints uses two related mechanisms:

1. **Resident reward mechanism:** Residents earn 1 EcoPoint for every kilogram of food waste successfully verified.
2. **Revenue allocation mechanism:** For every RM5 of eligible fertiliser sales revenue, 1 EcoPoint is allocated to the reward pool under the proposed funding model.

The resident reward mechanism determines how residents earn points. The revenue allocation mechanism is intended to help fund those rewards.

These mechanisms must be financially reconciled to ensure the programme remains sustainable. In particular, management must define the monetary value of redeemed points, the treatment of unsold fertiliser, and how operating costs affect the available reward pool.

## 🌏 Expected Impact

EcoPoints aims to deliver several environmental, social, and economic benefits:

- **Environmental:** Less organic waste sent to landfills and greater resource recovery.
- **Social:** Increased resident participation in sustainable living.
- **Economic:** Potential revenue from organic fertiliser sales and a funding source for community improvements.
- **Community Development:** Stronger collaboration between residents, workers, and property management.

## 🚧 Current Considerations

The following aspects remain to be finalised:

- Location and number of food waste collection centres.
- Food waste weighing equipment and recording procedures.
- Resident identification and submission verification methods.
- Rules for correcting inaccurate submissions and preventing duplicate claims.
- Point redemption values and eligible bill categories.
- Fertiliser processing arrangements and transportation schedules.
- Revenue allocation, operating costs, and reward pool sustainability.
- User roles, access permissions, and system security.

## 🚀 Vision

EcoPoints envisions a future where food waste is no longer treated as rubbish, but as a resource that creates value for the community.

By connecting sustainable waste management with a practical reward system, EcoPoints seeks to make environmentally responsible behaviour more accessible, rewarding, and sustainable.
